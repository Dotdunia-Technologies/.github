## How we work

We combine human thinking with AI-assisted workflows.

Our product process is:

1. **Understand** — the business, users and problem
2. **Explore** — possible ideas and directions
3. **Design** — turn the idea into something clear and testable
4. **Build** — develop the working product
5. **Connect** — integrate APIs, CRM, payments, WhatsApp and data
6. **Test** — real devices, browsers and human review
7. **Launch** — deploy and go live
8. **Improve** — learn from usage and keep improving

> AI speeds up the work. People give it direction.
